# ADR 0003: Position as backend + AI engineer; drop heavy 3D from v1
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** Resume shows Python/Django/DRF/Docker/CI-CD; AI being added. No frontend or 3D experience listed. Backend recruiters value deployed, documented systems over WebGL.
**Decision:** Design around the "API response" hero and case studies. Three.js, cannon-es and Blender are removed from v1; a small 3D element may be revisited later.
**Consequences:** Zero client JS on the home page, faster delivery, content matches the resume. Supersedes the 3D parts of ADR 0002.
